One of the main challenges I had in this assignment was using XPath selectors.
They felt harder and less clear to me than CSS selectors.
I struggled to build the right paths to reach certain elements,
especially when the elements were nested, didn’t have clear class names,
or were hidden by styles (display:none or visibility:hidden).

To solve this, I used the browser’s developer tools to check the page structure
and tried different XPath expressions.
I also tested my XPath in online tools and added print statements in my code
to make sure I was getting the right elements.
For hidden elements, I sometimes had to perform actions like clicking a button or scrolling
to make them visible before selecting them.
With practice, I began to understand how to use XPath better.
